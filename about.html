Welcome to Jane Austen Academy

Leading IGCSE & Edexcel Tuition in Sri Lanka

Jane Austen Academy is a leading provider of IGCSE, Edexcel, and Cambridge O-Level tuition in Sri Lanka. Established in 2014 in Panadura as Invictus School of English and Literature, the academy has grown into a respected international syllabus tuition centre.

Since its inception, the academy has maintained a strong record of academic excellence, producing A* and A grades in English Language and English Literature. These achievements reflect a commitment to effective teaching, structured exam preparation, and high academic standards.

Growth & Branches

Today, Jane Austen Academy is supported by 15 qualified teachers and 3 non-academic staff, offering Edexcel IGCSE, AS, and A2 programmes. The academy operates across Nugegoda, Panadura, and Ratnapura, serving a diverse student community.

The opening of the main branch in Nugegoda in November 2022 marked a key milestone in the academy’s expansion. Currently, over 300 students are enrolled across multiple subjects.

Commitment

Jane Austen Academy is dedicated to delivering high-quality international syllabus education, helping students develop confidence, academic excellence, and long-term success.
